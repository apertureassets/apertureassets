About

A finance brain,
a camera,
and not enough annual leave.

Alex

I’m a full-time finance professional with a passion for photography and an ever-growing list of destinations I haven’t been to yet. Aperture Assets is where those two things meet.

I’ve visited 15 countries and 7 US states. Every trip has been planned around a budget, squeezed into annual leave, and written up honestly.

Planning a trip is more work than it looks. You find a destination on social media, it pulls you in, and then you spend hours piecing together blogs and itineraries that tell you where to go but rarely what it actually costs, whether the reality matches the hype, or whether it’s genuinely worth using precious annual leave on.

That’s the gap Aperture Assets exists to fill. I tell you what I spent, what I’d skip, what surprised me, and whether I’d go back. No sponsored gloss, no perfect trips. Just an honest account from someone who has to make the same call you do: is this destination worth the days off?

As a finance professional, numbers are at the heart of every decision I make. That applies to travel as much as anything else. Every guide on this site includes a full cost breakdown and a genuine verdict, because that’s what I always wished I could find when I was doing the planning myself.
